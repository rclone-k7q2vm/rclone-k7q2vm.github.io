Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This app is a private rclone instance used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

It uses Google Drive access only to copy the household's own documents from its home server to its own Google Drive. No data is shared with, sold to or seen by anyone else, and nothing is stored anywhere other than that home server and that Google account.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
